Privacy Policy & Google API Services Disclosure

Effective Date: 8 September 2026

1. Introduction & Who We Are

Welcome to Tribe Family Hub (accessible at https://tribefamilyhub.uk, "Tribe", "we", "us", or "our"). Tribe provides an all-in-one family command centre designed to help households seamlessly manage shared calendars, coordinate chores, plan meals, extract actionable items from school communications, and keep the whole family organised in a secure, private environment.

We are committed to the highest standards of privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, protect, and handle your personal data and your family's information, with particular emphasis on our integration with third-party providers such as Google Workspace APIs.

If you have questions about this policy or our data practices, please contact us via the in-app Help & Feedback portal in Settings on your dashboard.

2. What We Collect & What We NEVER Keep

We believe in radical simplicity and privacy. Here is the straightforward guide to how your family's data works in Tribe:

What We Collect:

  • Basic Account Details: Just your name and email address when you sign in (e.g. via Google Sign-In) to verify your account and household membership. We do not collect or store your profile photo.
  • Items You Choose to Save: Only the specific events, grocery items, chores, meal plans, and notes you actively create or approve to save on your family board.
  • Device Reminders: Push notification tokens and basic device type solely so Tribe can deliver alerts and reminders to your phone or tablet.

What We NEVER Collect, Store, or Retain (Processed in Real Time, Then Immediately Discarded):

When you use Tribe's smart features (such as scanning a school newsletter, photographing a club timetable, or speaking a quick reminder), your raw data is processed in real time and immediately discarded without being stored:

  • No Photos or Document Scans Kept: Photos taken with your camera (e.g. school letters, flyers, recipe cards, pantry shelves) are read in volatile memory for one-time text extraction and immediately deleted. We never save, store, or archive your photos or images on our servers.
  • No Voice Recordings Kept: Voice dictations are transcribed into text on the fly and immediately discarded. We never keep audio recordings.
  • No Email Messages Stored: When scanning for school events or club fixtures, Tribe only extracts the specific date, time, and title of the activity. We do not store, copy, or retain your emails or personal messages.
  • No Profile Photos: We do not store Google or third-party profile pictures.

3. Where Your Data Lives & Why It Is 100% Secure

All Tribe Family Hub data is hosted directly on Google Cloud's own enterprise-grade Firebase servers located in London, UK (europe-west2).

  • Google's World-Leading Infrastructure: Your family's data is safeguarded by Google's multi-layered physical data centre security, round-the-clock monitoring, and automatic encryption both in transit and at rest.
  • Strictly Private to Your Household: Your family workspace is walled off and completely isolated. No other users or households can ever view your family's items.
  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your family's personal data to anyone, ever.
  • No Advertising: We never display ads, sell ad space, or use your family's data for profiling or advertising.
  • No AI Model Training: Your family's personal notes, schedules, and photos are never used to train public AI models.
  • Encrypted Connections: When you connect an email or calendar account (Google, Outlook, Yahoo), access credentials are encrypted with bank-level AES-256 encryption and used strictly for the sync features you choose.

4. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

Google API Disclosure

Tribe integrates directly with Google services to provide calendar synchronisation and account sign-in. When you connect your Google Account, we request access to specific scopes:

4.1 Requested Scopes & Purposes

  • openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile: Used strictly to authenticate your identity, create your user account, and display your name in your family workspace. We do not collect or store your profile photo.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar: Used strictly to synchronise events between Tribe Family Hub and your Google Calendar so you never double-book appointments.

4.2 Adherence to Google API Services User Data Policy

Limited Use Disclosure: Tribe Family Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy: Tribe Family Hub strictly complies with the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy. We do not use, transfer, or sell Google user data (including Google Calendar entries, whether raw, aggregated, or derived) to create, train, or improve foundational or generalised machine learning and artificial intelligence models.

4.3 Strict Prohibitions & Human Access Restrictions

  • No Sale or Sharing: We never sell or transfer Google user data to third parties.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • No AI Model Training & Complete Data Isolation: Google user data is never used to train, retrain, or fine-tune artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Tribe does not transfer Google Workspace user data to any third-party AI models or external model providers. All AI processing within Tribe is handled ephemerally via Google's own Gemini API on Google Cloud under commercial zero-data-retention terms where customer data is not used for generalised model training.
  • Zero Human Access: No humans read or view your Google user data or calendar events, except in rare instances where you provide explicit written consent for support diagnostics, or where strictly required by law.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not share your family's data with any external companies, except with vetted infrastructure providers strictly necessary to deliver the Tribe platform:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: For secure database hosting (Cloud Firestore), authentication, and notifications. Located in European data centres (europe-west2, London, UK).
  • Stripe: For secure payment processing for Tribe Premium subscriptions. We never process or store payment card numbers on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy & Family Safeguarding

Tribe is designed for households and families. We take special care to safeguard the privacy of younger family members:

  • Accounts must be created and administered by a parent or legal guardian aged 18 or older.
  • Profiles created for children within a family hub (e.g. for chore charts and school timetables) are managed exclusively under parental oversight within the private family partition.
  • We do not market to children, profile children, or share family hub information with public networks.
  • We adhere to the principles of the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code) and COPPA.

7. Data Retention, Revocation & Account Deletion

You have absolute control over your personal data and connected integrations:

7.1 Revoking Google Access

You can revoke Tribe's access to your Google account at any time without contacting us by visiting your Google Account Security Permissions Page and selecting "Remove Access" for Tribe.

7.2 In-App Account Deletion (Full Data Wipe)

You can permanently delete your Tribe account directly within the application:

  1. Open Tribe and navigate to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the prompt.

Upon confirmation, our automated Cloud Functions immediately purge your personal profile, authentication credentials, and encrypted OAuth tokens from our active database. If you are the sole member of your family hub, the entire family workspace (events, notes, tasks, meal plans) is permanently destroyed.

7.3 Manual Deletion Requests

If you are unable to access your account, you can submit a support ticket via the Help & Feedback portal on your dashboard, or delete your account directly at any time via Settings > Danger Zone.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR & Data Protection Laws

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018, you possess rights including:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request complete deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Restrict how we process your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain a structured, machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent for integrations (e.g. disconnecting Google integrations) at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a request through the in-app Help & Feedback portal on your dashboard.
