## 2. Feature-by-Feature Content Ideas & Scripts

### 2.1 The Sunday 8:00 PM School Circular Panic
*Featuring: **Multi-Provider Email Sync** (Google, Microsoft Outlook, Yahoo, Apple iCloud) & **AI Smart Convert Engine***

* **The Problem:** Schools, clubs, and sports teams send 4-page PDF newsletters and emails on Friday afternoon. By Sunday evening, parents are frantically scrolling trying to remember what day is non-uniform day, who needs swimming kit, and when the next inset day falls.
* **Content Hook:** *"POV: It's 8:30 PM on Sunday and you're reading a 600-word school circular trying to figure out if tomorrow is PE kit or World Book Day."*
* **Video Format (TikTok / Instagram Reel / YouTube Shorts):**
  1. **Hook (0-3s):** Stressed parent sitting on sofa staring at a chaotic school newsletter on their phone.
  2. **Agitation (3-7s):** Zoom in on dense paragraphs of text: cake sale on Thursday, non-uniform day on Friday, inset day next Monday, slip return deadline Tuesday.
  3. **The Solution (7-15s):** Open Tribe. Tap "Convert to Plan of Attack". Watch the AI instantly parse the newsletter into:
     - 🗓️ *Friday: Non-uniform day (£1 donation)*
     - 🗓️ *Next Monday: Inset Day (No school)*
     - 🎒 *Task assigned to Mum: Pack swimming kit Thursday*
     - 🛒 *Item added to shopping list: Plain flour for cake sale*
  4. **Call to Action (15-20s):** *"Tribe syncs with your Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, or Apple Mail and does the reading for you. Link in bio to try it free for 14 days — no card needed."*

### 2.2 The "Hands-Free Pram & Cooking Lifesaver"
*Featuring: **Magic Mic & Voice Dictation***

* **The Problem:** Parents have their best ideas or sudden reminders when their hands are completely tied: pushing a pram in the rain, driving, or elbows-deep in Bolognese sauce at the kitchen counter.
* **Content Hook:** *"Stop sending your partner frantic 3-minute WhatsApp voice notes they will never listen to."*
* **Video Script / Audio Concept:**
  - **Visual:** Parent holding a crying toddler or stirring a pot on the hob with one hand, tapping the floating microphone in Tribe.
  - **Natural Voice Input:** *"Remind Dave to pickup Oliver from football on Wednesday at 6, Sophie needs her recorder for primary school on Thursday, and add halloumi, sourdough, and oat milk to the shopping list."*
  - **AI Transformation:** Screen shows Tribe listening, thinking for 1 second, and cleanly splitting the sentence into:
    - 2 separate calendar events colour-coded by child
    - 1 reminder assigned to Dave
    - 3 grocery items automatically sorted into their respective supermarket aisles
  - **On-Screen Text:** *"One sentence. Everything sorted. Zero typing."*

### 2.3 The Crumpled School Flyer Graveyard
*Featuring: **Smart Camera OCR / Document Scanner***

* **The Problem:** The crumpled piece of paper fished out from the bottom of a school rucksack behind a bruised banana and a half-drunk water bottle.
* **Content Hook:** *"Parents: The bottom-of-the-schoolbag paper graveyard stops here."*
* **Carousel / Before-and-After Video:**
  - **Slide 1 / Before:** Photo of a creased, stained paper flyer for the summer fete or an after-school karate club registration.
  - **Slide 2 / Action:** Snapping a quick photo inside Tribe's Smart Capture camera.
  - **Slide 3 / After:** AI extracts the date, time, location, payment deadline, and dress code, instantly generating calendar entries and chore reminders.
  - **Caption:** *"You don't need a magnetic fridge covered in 50 curling pieces of paper. Snap a photo in Tribe, bin the paper, and get on with your evening."*

### 2.4 The Supermarket Aisle Peacemaker
*Featuring: **1-Tap Aisle Shopping List with Store Section Grouping***

* **The Problem:** Sending a partner to the supermarket with an unordered text list results in 12 phone calls from the aisles (*"Where is the double cream?", "What section is cumin in?"*), or wandering from aisle 2 to aisle 14 and back again.
* **Content Hook:** *"How to cut 20 minutes off your weekly grocery shop and save your marriage."*
* **Meme / Skit Concept:**
  - **Side A (Without Tribe):** Partner wandering aimlessly between the bakery and frozen foods looking at a messy notes app, sweating, calling partner repeatedly.
  - **Side B (With Tribe):** Partner smoothly walking down each aisle in order. Items are automatically grouped into **Produce & Greengrocer**, **Bakery**, **Meat & Butcher**, **Chilled & Dairy**, and **Pantry**.
  - **Bonus Feature Highlight:** Live synchronisation. When someone at home adds bread to the list, it immediately pops up on the shopper's phone while they are in the bakery section.

  - **Slide 3:** Tap "Add Ingredients to Shopping List" — all required items flow directly into your aisle list without duplicate entries.
  - **Slide 4:** Dietary filters (Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Quick Prep, Kid-Friendly).

## 5. Campaign Calendar & Launch Cadence

* **Sunday Evenings (The "Admin Reset" Campaign):**  
  Post content targeting the weekly reset: school circulars, weekly meal planning, and calendar clash checks before Monday morning.
* **Mid-Week (The "Relatable Chaos" Campaign):**  
  Humorous, light-hearted reels highlighting lost PE kits, forgotten dinner ingredients, and chaotic pickups.
* **School Term Milestones (High-Intent Spikes):**  
  - *Back to School (September & January):* Focus on school calendar sync, uniform checklists, and after-school club coordination.
  - *Half Term & Holidays (October, February, May):* Focus on activity planning, meal planning, and coordinating holiday childcare.
  - *Summer Term Inset Days & Fetes (June–July):* Managing school notices, teacher gifts, sports days, and picnic supplies.
