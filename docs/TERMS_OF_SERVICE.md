### 4.2 Statutory HMRC Record Keeping Requirements (5 to 6 Year Rule)
Under United Kingdom tax legislation (including the Taxes Management Act 1970 s12B and the Value Added Tax Act 1994 Sch 11):
- Sole traders and self-employed individuals must retain original financial records and receipts for at least **5 years** from the 31 January submission deadline of the relevant tax year.
- Limited companies must retain statutory accounting records for at least **6 years** from the end of the financial year.
- **Tribe Trade Does Not Store Receipt Images:** For privacy, security, and data minimisation, all receipt photos and images are processed ephemerally in volatile memory and are permanently discarded immediately upon analysis. Tribe Trade is not a document archive. You are legally required to keep your original paper receipts or independent digital backups for the statutory HMRC period.
