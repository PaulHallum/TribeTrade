## 1. Trade Crew Onboarding: Inviting Your Team

TribeTrade is built around a shared **Trade Hub**. This means sole traders, partners, apprentices, and office managers can all share the exact same job calendar, materials pick lists, van stock, and site whiteboard notes.

Here is how to invite an apprentice or subbie to your Trade Hub:

1. Open TribeTrade and tap **Settings** (the gear icon in the navigation bar).
2. Scroll to the **Crew & Team Settings** section.
3. Your unique **6-Digit Crew Join Code** (e.g. `482 910`) is displayed.
4. Have your team member open TribeTrade on their phone or tablet.
5. On first launch, they simply tap **Join Existing Trade Hub**.
6. Enter the 6-digit code, and their device is immediately connected to your hub!

*Tip: Tap **Share Crew Link** to send the invitation directly via WhatsApp or SMS.*

### Quick Navigation: The TribeTrade Home Pill & Shimmer Menu Button
TribeTrade provides swift, tactile navigation from any screen:
- **TribeTrade Home Pill:** Located in the top-left corner, the high-contrast branded pill embeds the company logo on a clean white surface with your trade accent colour, providing 1-tap navigation directly back to your Trade Hub from any screen.
- **Shimmer Menu Button:** Located in the top-right corner, an eye-catching themed button with a dynamic light-beam shimmer opens the full **Navigation Drawer** with 1-tap access to your Hub, Calendar, Quotes & Invoices, Expenses, Tasks, The Shed Supplies, Email, Settings, and User Guide.

## 2. The AI Sandbox: A Safe Place to Test

**The answer is: No!**

When you launch the **Interactive Dashboard Tour** (available in Settings > Help & Guides or at `/try`), you are in an **AI Sandbox**:

- **Learn Without Fear:** Tap any action, simulate quotes, convert to invoices, and scan receipts. When the tour finishes, your live workspace remains completely clean.

## 3. Work Tablet PIN Security & Session Lock

TribeTrade is frequently installed on a dedicated tablet kept in the van or workshop. To protect client phone numbers, quotes, and HMRC financial records from unauthorised hands, you can enable an optional 4-digit PIN lock.

**How to set up the PIN on a shared work device:**
1. Tap **Settings** > **App Preferences & Security**.
2. Toggle on the **PIN Lock** feature.
3. Enter a memorable 4-digit PIN. From then on, reopening the app or waking the screen requires your 4-digit PIN.

**Forgot PIN or Locked Out?**
1. On the PIN entry screen, tap **Sign Out** / **Forgot PIN?**.
2. You will be redirected to the secure login screen. Sign in using your registered Google, Microsoft, or Apple account.
3. Once authenticated, the old PIN is disabled and you regain full access. You can configure a new PIN in Settings.

## 7. Trade Quote Management & Business Profile

Located directly next to **Calendar** in the main navigation, **Quotes** provides a fast, tactile quotation manager designed for busy UK tradespeople:

### Setting Up Your Business Details
Before sharing your first quote, go to **Settings** > **Business Details & Quotation Defaults**:
1. Enter your **Trading / Company Name** and full **Trading Address**.
2. Add your contact phone number, email, and website.
3. If applicable, enter your **Company Registration Number** and toggle **VAT Registered** with your **VAT Number** and default UK VAT rate (20%).
4. Specify your standard **Hourly Rate** (e.g. £45/hr) and **Day Rate** (e.g. £320/day) for quick one-tap pricing.
5. Set your **BACS Bank Details** (Bank Name, Account Name, Sort Code, Account Number) and default payment terms. These are automatically formatted onto all client-facing PDF quotes and invoice summaries.

### Creating & Managing Quotes
1. Tap the **Quotes** tab in the top navigation.
2. Use the top toggle to switch between **Quotes** and **Invoices** (just like Tasks & Notes).
3. Tap **+ New Quote**.
4. Fill in customer details and multiline job scope (rendered in full on the A4 PDF export without truncation).
5. Tap **+ Add Labour** to enter precise duration using dedicated **Days** and **Hours** fields (e.g. 1 day and 3 hours @ Day Rate), or **+ Add Material / Consumable** with preset units (`litres`, `tins`, `pack`, `bags`, `metres`, `sq m`, `rolls`, `units`, or custom).
6. View live calculations of Labour Subtotal, Materials Subtotal, Net Subtotal, VAT (if registered), and Grand Total in British Pounds (£).
7. Tap **Save Draft** or **Preview & Share**.

### Sequential Monotonic Numbering (Never Resets)
Quotes and invoices follow a strict sequential numbering system (`Q-1001`, `Q-1002`, `INV-1001`, `INV-1002`):
- **Never Resets on Deletion:** If you delete a quote or invoice (e.g. deleting `Q-1002`), the sequence never rewinds or re-uses that number. Your next quote will reliably be `Q-1003`, eliminating the danger of duplicate numbers or turning up at the wrong job with the same reference.
- **Tracked in Business Profile:** TribeTrade stores your highest issued quote and invoice sequence numbers in your cloud profile (`highestQuoteNumber` and `highestInvoiceNumber`), guaranteeing numbering integrity across all devices.

### Job Acceptance & Smart Convert: Calendar Booking, Shed Check & Invoicing
When a customer accepts a quotation (or when you mark it as **Accepted**), TribeTrade launches the **Job Won & Accepted** Smart Convert workflow:
1. **Calendar Booking:** Automatically calculates estimated job duration from the labour items and suggests dates. With one click, book the job into your calendar complete with client contact details and site address.
2. **The Shed Stock Cross-Reference:** Automatically compares required materials against your real-time **Shed Inventory**. Items in stock are flagged with an **In Shed** badge showing available quantity.
3. **Shopping Pick List Integration:** Items not in the shed (or needing ordering) can be selected and added directly to your **Trade Supplies / Shopping Pick List** with supplier lookup links (Screwfix, Toolstation, Travis Perkins).
4. **Convert to Invoice:** 1-tap conversion turns the accepted quote into a sequential tax invoice (e.g. `INV-1001`) with automatic payment due dates and identical vector A4 PDF layout.

### Trade Invoices & Payment Tracking
- Toggle to the **Invoices** view from the top of the Quotes page.
- Track **Total Invoiced**, **Paid Invoices**, and **Outstanding Balance** in real-time.
- Update payment statuses (`Draft`, `Sent`, `Paid`, `Completed`, `Overdue`).
- **Completed & Paid Automatic Turnover Sync:** Marking an invoice as **Completed** or **Paid** automatically logs an income record in your Trade Expenses ledger under HMRC Box 10 (Turnover), including net, VAT, and gross breakdown, keeping your Self-Assessment tax pot up to date.
- **Effortless Modal Exit:** Invoice and quote modals support instant dismissal via backdrop tap, the Escape key, or dedicated Close buttons.

## 8. Expenses, Receipt Scanning & HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD)

Located immediately next to **Quotes** in the navigation bar, the **Expenses** tab tracks all trade purchases, materials, subcontractor invoices, and customer turnover for UK tax and accounting compliance.

### How to Record Receipts and Business Purchases
1. **Scan Receipt (Camera or Gallery):**
   - Tap **Scan Receipt** (or tap the camera icon next to Magic Mic while on the Expenses tab).
   - Take a clear photo of your paper merchant till slip, fuel receipt, or invoice.
   - Tribe's AI automatically extracts the merchant name (e.g. Screwfix, Travis Perkins, Shell), date, gross amount, VAT rate (20%, 5%, 0%), VAT amount, net amount, and reference number.
   - The AI automatically categorises the purchase into official **HMRC SA103 / MTD expense categories** (e.g. *Materials & Goods*, *Tools & Plant Hire*, *Van & Fuel*, *Subcontractor Labour*).
   - Review and verify the extracted figures in the review editor, then tap **Save Record**.
2. **Manual Transaction Entry:**
   - Tap **Manual Entry** to log cash purchases, customer invoice payments, bank transfers, or trade account transactions.
   - Switch between **Expense / Purchase** and **Income / Sales**.
   - Input the gross amount and VAT rate; Net and VAT amounts calculate automatically.

### HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) CSV Export
- Located directly below the transaction entry ledger (and above the statutory compliance notices), tap **Export MTD CSV**.
- Filter by **Current Tax Year (6 April - 5 April)**, **Previous Tax Year**, or **Quarterly Periods** using the tax period selector.
- Tribe generates an accountant-ready CSV complete with HMRC SA103 box classifications (Box 11, Box 12, Box 14, etc.), VAT breakdown, net profit/loss calculations, and transaction references.
- Send the CSV directly to your bookkeeper or import it into HMRC MTD bridging software.

### Sole Trader Self Assessment Preparation Assistant
Located right next to the MTD export button, tap **Self Assessment Helper** to open an interactive preparation assistant:
- **Disclaimer & Non-Tax Tool Notice:** This assistant is strictly for planning and organisation. It is **NOT** a formal tax filing engine, chartered tax advice, or an HMRC submission portal. Always verify final figures with a qualified accountant.
- **HMRC SA103 Box-by-Box Breakdown:** Automatically maps your recorded income and expenses into official Self Assessment form boxes (Box 10 Turnover, Box 11 Materials & Subcontractors, Box 12 Motor Expenses, Box 14 Tools/Plant, Box 16 Phone/Office, Box 19 Trade Insurance/Fees, Box 28 Total Expenses, Box 31 Net Profit).
- **Estimated Tax & NI Liability:** Calculates indicative Income Tax (using Personal Allowance £12,570 and 20%/40% basic/higher rates) and Class 4 National Insurance (6%/2%).
- **CIS (Construction Industry Scheme) Deductions Tracker:** Trade subcontractors can input the 20% CIS tax withheld at source by main contractors to see their true net balance payable or estimated HMRC tax refund.
- **Recommended Monthly Savings Pot:** Suggests an estimated amount to set aside each month into a separate business savings account to prevent 31 January tax panic.
- **Payments on Account Preview:** Outlines estimated 50% advance payments if tax liability exceeds £1,000.
- **Copy / Share for Accountant:** 1-tap **Copy for Accountant** creates a clean, professional summary ready to email or WhatsApp directly to your bookkeeper or accountant.

### Important: Statutory Financial Notices & HMRC Record-Keeping Footers
- **AI Tool Disclaimer:** Both the **Quotes & Invoices** and **Expenses & Bookkeeping** ledgers feature unobtrusive footer notices reminding users that TribeTrade is an AI assistant and productivity tool, not a certified accountant or registered tax adviser. All figures, VAT estimates, CIS deductions, and MTD totals must be checked prior to submission.
- **Ephemeral Image Processing:** To protect your privacy and reduce storage costs, **receipt images are never stored in the database or cloud storage**. Images are processed in short-lived memory by AI and immediately discarded.
- **5 to 6 Year Physical Receipt Rule:** Under UK tax legislation (TMA 1970 s12B / VATA 1994), sole traders must keep original physical or digital records for at least **5 years** and limited companies for **6 years** from the 31 January tax return deadline. **Always retain your original paper receipts or personal digital copies for HMRC inspection.**
- **Verify Extracted Numbers:** Optical character recognition (OCR) can occasionally misread faded till slips or thermal paper. Always check and verify extracted numbers before saving.

## 9. Vehicle & Transport Fleet Compliance

Keeping work vans, pick-ups, and trade transport road-legal is critical. TribeTrade features a dedicated compliance tracker built directly into **Settings** and automatically connected to your **Calendar**.

### Tracking Transport Due Dates
1. Open **Settings** > **Vehicle & Transport Fleet Compliance**.
2. Tap **Add Vehicle** and enter your vehicle's nickname (e.g. *Primary Work Van*), UK registration plate (e.g. *VA21 XYZ*), and vehicle type (*Panel Van*, *Pick-up Truck*, *Tipper*, *Car*, *Trailer*, etc.).
3. For each vehicle, configure your renewal due dates:
   - **MOT** (Ministry of Transport test)
   - **Vehicle Insurance**
   - **Annual Servicing**
   - **Road Tax (VED)**
   - **Custom Items:** Tap **Add Custom Check** for transport specifics like *LOLER / Tail Lift Inspection*, *Tachograph Calibration*, or *Breakdown Cover*.
4. Enter optional garage or policy notes (e.g. policy number, local testing station).
5. Tap **Save Fleet Changes**.

### Automatic Calendar Integration & 1-Month Reminders
Because transport deadlines are configured in **Settings**, they are **forced into your calendar automatically every single year**:
- **Entry 1: 1-Month Advance Reminder:** Exactly 1 calendar month before the due date, an amber reminder entry is posted (e.g. `⚠️ 1 Month Reminder: MOT Due - Primary Work Van (VA21 XYZ)`). This provides sufficient advance notice to book an MOT slot or shop around for insurance renewals.
- **Entry 2: Due Date:** On the actual deadline, a red alert entry appears (e.g. `🚨 MOT DUE TODAY - Primary Work Van (VA21 XYZ)`).
- **Annual Recurrence:** Whether browsing 2026, 2027, or 2028, compliance dates automatically recur annually without requiring manual re-entry.

### 1-Click Renewal (+1 Year)
When your MOT passes or your van completes its annual service:
- Open the entry in your Calendar and tap **Renew for Next Year (+1 Yr)** (or tap **+1 Yr** in Settings).
- Tribe instantly pushes the due date forward by exactly 1 year and updates the calendar.

## 14. Multi-Device Protection & Device Swapping (Max 4 Devices)

To protect account security and prevent excessive account sharing, TribeTrade enforces an automated multi-device management policy:

### 13.1 Device Allocation Rules
- **4 Active Devices:** Each account can be actively logged into up to **4 registered devices** concurrently (for example: 2 mobile phones, a van tablet, and a home office laptop).
- **Automatic Registration:** When you sign in on a new device, it is automatically registered to an available slot if you have fewer than 4 devices logged in.

### 13.2 Anti-Churn Fail-Safe (2 Changes per Month)
- To prevent accounts being shared across dozens of rotating devices, users are permitted up to **2 device replacements / swaps per rolling 30-day period**.
- If you attempt to log in on a 5th device:
  - If you have device swaps remaining, the **Device Gate** will appear, allowing you to select an old device to remove and replace with your current device.
  - If your 2 monthly swaps are exhausted, the Device Gate displays an informative notice showing the exact date your next device change will unlock.
- **Managing Devices in Settings:** View all currently registered devices, see which device is currently active, and inspect remaining monthly swaps under **Settings > Registered Devices**.

### 13.3 Admin Device Reset
- If you are the account administrator, a **Reset Devices (Admin)** button appears on the Device Gate screen alongside the standard Recheck and Sign Out controls, and also at the bottom of the **Settings > Accounts > Registered Devices** section.
- Tapping this button clears **all** registered device slots and resets the monthly swap history to zero, allowing you to start fresh.
- This is useful when testing on multiple devices or when a clean slate is needed without waiting for swaps to regenerate.

### 13.4 Admin Subscription Override
- If you are the account administrator and your subscription is not currently Premium, a **Force Set Premium (Admin Override)** button appears in the **Settings > Billing > Subscription & Plan** section.
- Tapping this button directly sets the `subscriptionTier` to `'premium'` on your `trade_users` Firestore document, bypassing Stripe.

## 16. Managing & Deleting Your Account

If you ever wish to permanently close your account and delete your stored trade business data:

1. Open **Settings** from the main menu.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page to the **Danger Zone** section.
3. Tap **Delete Account & Data**.
4. Confirm the warning popup.
5. TribeTrade will immediately purge your business data, quotes, invoices, and expenses from Firestore, remove your user record from Firebase Authentication, and sign you out cleanly.
