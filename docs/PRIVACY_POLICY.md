# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 8 September 2026

Welcome to **Tribe Family Hub** (accessible at [tribefamilyhub.uk](https://tribefamilyhub.uk), "Tribe", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the highest standards of data security for your family. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information, with a specific focus on our third-party integrations (including Google Workspace APIs) and AI processing capabilities.

## 1. What We Collect & What We NEVER Keep

We believe in radical simplicity and privacy. Here is the straightforward guide to how your family's data works in Tribe:

### What We Collect:
- **Basic Account Details:** Just your name and email address when you sign in (e.g. via Google Sign-In) to verify your account and household membership. We do **not** collect or store your profile photo.
- **Items You Choose to Save:** Only the specific events, grocery items, chores, meal plans, and notes you actively create or approve to save on your family board.
- **Device Reminders:** Push notification tokens and basic device type solely so Tribe can deliver alerts and reminders to your phone or tablet.

### What We NEVER Collect, Store, or Retain (Processed in Real Time, Then Immediately Discarded):
When you use Tribe's smart features (such as scanning a school newsletter, photographing a club timetable, or speaking a quick reminder), your raw data is processed **in real time and immediately discarded without being stored**:
- **No Photos or Document Scans Kept:** Photos taken with your camera (e.g. school letters, flyers, recipe cards, pantry shelves) are read in volatile memory for one-time text extraction and immediately deleted. We never save, store, or archive your photos or images on our servers.
- **No Voice Recordings Kept:** Voice dictations are transcribed into text on the fly and immediately discarded. We never keep audio recordings.
- **No Email Messages Stored:** When scanning for school events or club fixtures, Tribe only extracts the specific date, time, and title of the activity. We do not store, copy, or retain your emails or personal messages.
- **No Profile Photos:** We do not store Google or third-party profile pictures.

## 2. Where Your Data Lives & Why It Is 100% Secure

All Tribe Family Hub data is hosted directly on **Google Cloud's own enterprise-grade Firebase servers located in London, UK (europe-west2)**.
- **Google's World-Leading Infrastructure:** Your family's data is safeguarded by Google's multi-layered physical data centre security, round-the-clock monitoring, and automatic encryption both in transit and at rest.
- **Strictly Private to Your Household:** Your family workspace is walled off and completely isolated. No other users or households can ever view your family's items.
- **No Selling of Data:** We never sell, rent, or trade your family's personal data to anyone, ever.
- **No Advertising:** We never display ads, sell ad space, or use your family's data for profiling or advertising.
- **No AI Model Training:** Your family's personal notes, schedules, and photos are never used to train public AI models.
- **Encrypted Connections:** When you connect an email or calendar account (Google, Outlook, Yahoo), access credentials are encrypted with bank-level AES-256 encryption and used strictly for the sync features you choose.

## 3. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes (Google API Services Disclosure)

To unify your family's scheduling and communication, Tribe Family Hub integrates directly with Google services. When you choose to connect your Google Account, we request the following OAuth scopes:

- **`openid`**, **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email`**, **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile`**: Strictly used to authenticate your identity, create your user account, and display your name in your private family workspace. We do not collect or store your profile photo.
- **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar`**: Strictly used to synchronise events between Tribe Family Hub and your primary Google Calendar, ensuring family members can view personal appointments alongside household events and sync Tribe events to Google Calendar.

### 3.1 Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

> **Tribe Family Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.**

> **Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy:** Tribe Family Hub strictly complies with the [Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy](https://developers.google.com/workspace/workspace-api-user-data-developer-policy). We do not use, transfer, or sell Google user data (including Google Calendar entries, whether raw, aggregated, or derived) to create, train, or improve foundational or generalised machine learning and artificial intelligence models.

### 3.2 Prohibitions on Data Sharing, Advertising & AI Training
- **No Sale of Data:** We **never sell, rent, or trade** Google user data or personal data to third parties.
- **No Advertising:** Google user data is **never used or transferred for advertising**, retargeting, or personalised ad delivery.
- **No AI Model Training & Complete Data Isolation:** We **never use Google user data, emails, or calendar entries to train, retrain, or fine-tune generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models** (including foundation LLMs). Tribe does not transfer Google Workspace user data to any third-party AI models or external model providers. All AI processing in Tribe is executed ephemerally via Google's own Gemini API on Google Cloud under commercial zero-data-retention terms where customer data is not used for generalised model training.
- **Strict Human Access Restrictions:** Tribe enforces strict controls prohibiting human access to your Google data, emails, or calendar events, except where you provide explicit written consent for support diagnostics or where required by law.

## 4. Children's Privacy & Family Safeguarding

Tribe is built for families and households:
- Accounts must be created and administered by parents or legal guardians aged 18 or older.
- Child profiles (e.g. for chore charts and school timetables) are managed exclusively under parental oversight within the private family workspace partition.
- Child data is never monetised, never used for advertising, and never shared publicly, adhering to the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and COPPA principles.

## 6. Account Deletion, Revocation & Data Retention

You maintain full control over your data and connected integrations:
- **Revoking Google Access:** You can revoke Tribe's access to your Google account at any time via your [Google Account Security Permissions Page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **In-App Deletion:** You may permanently delete your Tribe account and all associated data at any time via **Settings > Delete Account**. Upon confirmation, our automated systems instantly wipe your personal profile, encrypted tokens, and (if you are the sole member) your entire shared family workspace.
- **Manual Deletion Requests:** You may also submit a request via the in-app **Help & Feedback** portal in Settings from your registered account, which will be processed in compliance with UK GDPR within 30 days.

## 7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your personal data, or withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, please submit a request via the in-app **Help & Feedback** portal on your dashboard.
